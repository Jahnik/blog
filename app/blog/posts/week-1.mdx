If you somehow missed the first page, I'll reiterate, this is a record of my time at the Fractal Tech Bootcamp. Described in the partially pararphrased words of its founder as a place for "smart people who kinda crashed out", it appears to be the perfect place for me. 

I assume that whoever is reading this (likely the instructors, since making/writing this is part of the program) will have some basic familiarity of what Fractal Bootcamp is, so I won't get into that and will continue directly into my thoughts and potentially inane ramblings on the program. If by some miracle you have no idea what the Fractal Tech Bootcamp is then I would direct you to their [website](https://fractalbootcamp.com/)

## So what was Week One like?

Week one was pretty much what I expected from their advertising and messaging, it was definitely the largest amount of sustained, intense work I have done in perhaps years. Roughly 11 hours a day of coding with minor breaks for lectures or to grab food is definitely not how I usually spend my time, but I can speak firsthand for the amount of knowledge and experience I was able to gather in that time, mostly due to the structure of the program. What I didnt expect was how effective their method of teaching was.

## What was the method?

They presented us with an assignment, gave a brief lecture on the broad structure of how we should approach the problem. From there most of the work was done independently, though we were all encouraged to ask chatGPT if we ran into any simple problems. The real shine of the program is that if you ever get blocked for more than say ten minutes, then you can raise your hand and one of the three instructors who are milling around the space will come and help you to get unblocked. It is important to note here that they won't solve your problem for you, but will instead provide you exactly the help you need to move past the problem. This loop of working as much as you can, getting additional info from the AI, and then getting the minimum info needed to move forward mirrors the process of development, and is incredibly effective for maintaining focus for long periods of time.
